替换产品后细节丢失 — 根因诊断与解法
专为「从 INS/Pinterest 找图 → 替换成自家相框」场景设计
🔴 常见失败症状 → 根本原因
颜色偏移
AI 受场景光线影响，将相框颜色「融合」进环境色温，导致橡木变黄、胡桃木变灰
框条变细/变粗
AI 根据场景中原有相框的比例「猜测」新相框，而非严格参照上传图
木纹纹理消失
AI 将纹理视为「噪点」进行平滑处理，尤其在低分辨率参考图时更严重
宽高比变形
AI 自动适配场景中原相框的透视角度，导致比例被重新计算
榫卯/五金细节丢失
细节特征在低分辨率或复杂背景下被 AI 忽略，生成「通用相框」而非你的产品
玻璃反光消失
AI 未收到明确指令保留玻璃材质，默认生成无反光的哑光表面
✅ 对应解法
解法1：属性清单锁定法
在提示词中逐条列出需要保留的属性，不能只说「保持产品不变」，要具体到每一个维度
解法2：上传高清产品图
参考图必须是白底正面高清图，分辨率越高细节保留越好。模糊图 = 细节丢失的起点
解法3：颜色精确描述
不要说「木色」，要说「warm medium-brown walnut with visible straight grain lines」，越具体越准
解法4：抗融合指令
加「do not adapt the frame color to the scene lighting」防止 AI 把颜色「融进」环境
解法5：两步法（先抠图再合成）
先让 AI 生成白底产品图，再做溶图合成，比直接替换细节保留率高 60%+
解法6：迭代追加修正
第一次生成后，不重新写提示词，直接追加「the frame color is too dark, restore it to [描述]」

🔑 终极公式 · 从 INS/Pinterest 图替换相框（细节全保留版） 上传2张图
// Image 1 = 从INS/Pinterest找到的场景图；Image 2 = 你的相框白底产品图

Use [Image 1] as the background scene. Replace the picture frame in the scene with the frame shown in [Image 2].

CRITICAL — Preserve ALL of the following product attributes EXACTLY as shown in Image 2:
— Frame width: [e.g. wide frame, approximately 2-inch molding]
— Wood color: [e.g. warm medium-brown walnut with reddish undertones]
— Wood grain: [e.g. straight, fine grain lines running horizontally]
— Surface finish: [e.g. satin, low-sheen, not glossy]
— Glass: [e.g. clear tempered glass with subtle specular reflection]
— Corner joints: [e.g. clean mitered corners with visible wood grain continuity]
— Aspect ratio: [e.g. portrait orientation, 5:7 ratio]

DO NOT:
— Do not adapt the frame color to match the scene's ambient lighting
— Do not smooth or remove the wood grain texture
— Do not alter the frame's width or proportions to match the original frame in the scene
— Do not change the glass reflection properties

Keep the background, wall, furniture, and all other scene elements exactly as in Image 1. The replaced frame should sit in the same position, angle, and scale as the original frame. Add a realistic shadow consistent with the scene lighting. Photorealistic result.
⚡ 两步法（细节保留率最高，推荐用于重要图片）
1
第一步：生成场景适配版产品图 先做这步
上传你的相框白底图，提示词：「Relight this product to match the lighting of [场景描述, e.g. a warm-toned living room with soft left-side window light]. Keep ALL product details identical — frame color, wood grain, proportions, glass. Only adjust the lighting and shadow direction. Output on transparent/white background.」
2
第二步：将重新打光的产品图合成进场景 再做这步
上传场景图 + 第一步生成的产品图，提示词：「Place [Image 2 - the product] into [Image 1 - the scene], replacing the existing frame. The product lighting already matches the scene. Blend seamlessly — add contact shadow, match perspective and scale. Do not alter the product in any way.」
🎨 KovaScape 相框颜色精确描述模板（直接复制使用）
胡桃木（Walnut）颜色描述 复制使用
Wood color: deep warm brown with dark chocolate undertones, medium-dark value, not black. Grain: straight to slightly wavy fine grain lines, subtle figuring. Surface: satin finish, low sheen, natural wood luster. The color should read as rich walnut brown (#4A2C17 range), not gray-brown or black.
橡木（Oak）颜色描述 复制使用
Wood color: light to medium warm honey-blonde, golden undertones, natural oak tone. Grain: prominent straight grain with occasional ray fleck pattern, medium-coarse texture. Surface: satin finish, natural wood warmth. The color should read as light natural oak (#C8A96E range), not yellow or orange.
迭代修正公式（生成后细节不对时追加） 追加指令
// 颜色偏了
The frame color in the result is too [dark/light/gray/yellow]. Restore it to [颜色描述]. Do not change anything else in the image.

// 木纹消失了
The wood grain texture on the frame is missing. Add back the [straight/wavy] grain lines characteristic of [walnut/oak]. Keep all other elements unchanged.

// 框条变细了
The frame molding is too narrow. The frame width should be [wider — approximately X inches wide]. Regenerate with the correct frame proportions while keeping the scene identical.

// 宽高比变了
The frame aspect ratio is wrong. It should be [portrait/landscape, X:Y ratio]. Correct the proportions without changing the scene or other product details.
1
替换产品
在已有场景图中换掉产品，保留背景、光线、构图不变

核心逻辑

上传「原始场景图」+ 「新产品图」，明确告诉 AI 哪个是背景锚点、哪个是要替换进去的主体。关键词：keep the background exactly as is、replace only the [产品]、match the original lighting and perspective。细节保留见上方专项章节。

上传原场景图 + 上传新产品图 → 指定替换对象 → 属性清单锁定 → 锁定背景/光线
公式 1-A · 场景内换产品（标准版） 上传2张图
Use [Image 1] as the background scene anchor. Replace the [原产品描述, e.g. picture frame on the wall] with the product shown in [Image 2].

Preserve from Image 2: exact shape, proportions, color, texture, material finish, and all surface details.
Keep from Image 1: background, wall, furniture, lighting direction, shadows, and overall composition — exactly as is.

The new product should sit naturally in the same position with matching perspective and scale. Blend seamlessly — photorealistic, no visible compositing artifacts.
公式 1-B · 纯白底换颜色/款式 上传1张图
Take the uploaded product image. Keep the exact shape, proportions, frame profile, corner joints, and overall design. Change only the [color / finish, e.g. frame color from oak to walnut] to [颜色精确描述].

Output on a pure white background, soft studio lighting, subtle drop shadow beneath the product. E-commerce packshot style. No props, no extra objects.
🎯
锁定背景的关键词

「keep the background exactly as is」「do not alter the scene」「preserve the original environment」

⚡
防止颜色被「融合」

「do not adapt the frame color to the scene's ambient lighting」— 这句话是防止颜色偏移的核心

2
局部修改
只改图片中的某一个元素，其余部分完全保留

核心逻辑

局部修改的成败在于「保留指令」的精确度。不仅要说清楚改什么，更要明确说「不要动什么」。使用 Edit only… + Do not change… 双重锁定结构，成功率大幅提升。

上传原图 → Edit only [目标元素] → 描述修改内容 → Do not change [保留内容]
公式 2-A · 换背景，保留产品 上传1张图
Edit the uploaded image. Change only the background to [新背景描述, e.g. a bright Scandinavian living room with white walls and natural wood furniture].

Do not change the product — preserve its exact shape, proportions, material texture, color, and position. Maintain consistent lighting direction. The product should look naturally placed in the new environment.
公式 2-B · 修改画芯内容（换相框内的图片） 上传1张图
Edit the uploaded image. Edit only the artwork/photo displayed inside the frame — replace it with [新画芯描述, e.g. a black and white fine art portrait photograph / a monstera leaf botanical line drawing].

Keep everything else identical: the frame itself (color, texture, width, profile), background, lighting, shadows, and overall composition must remain exactly as in the original image. The new artwork should appear naturally behind the glass, with the same glass reflection.
公式 2-C · 移除/添加元素 上传1张图
Edit the uploaded image. Remove the [要移除的元素, e.g. price tag / watermark / prop on the left] from the scene. Fill the area naturally to match the surrounding background — no visible artifacts or blurring.

Do not alter any other part of the image.
⚠️ 常见失败原因

只说「改背景」而不说「保留产品」，AI 会同时重绘产品。务必用「Do not change the product」明确锁定。如果产品细节被改动，追加：「The frame in the result must match the original upload exactly — same color, same wood grain, same width.」

3
溶图合成
将多张图片融合为一张，光线、色调、风格统一自然

核心逻辑

溶图最大的挑战是「割裂感」——产品像贴上去的。解决方案：① 明确指定主体图和背景图的层级关系；② 使用 harmonize 指令统一光线色温；③ 要求生成「realistic contact shadow」让产品「落地」；④ 相框溶图时额外声明「preserve frame color and texture」防止颜色被融合。

公式 3-A · 相框融入场景（防细节丢失版） 上传2张图
Composite the frame from [Image 2] into the scene from [Image 1].

Place the frame at [位置, e.g. the center of the wall / above the console table]. Harmonize the global illumination and shadow direction so the frame appears to exist in the same physical space — but do NOT change the frame's intrinsic color or wood grain texture during harmonization. Only adjust the lighting highlights and shadows, not the base material color.

Add a realistic cast shadow on the wall behind the frame. Maintain the frame's original proportions and all surface details. Photorealistic, magazine-quality result.
公式 3-B · 多框组合展示（gallery wall） 上传多张图
Create a gallery wall arrangement using the frames from the uploaded images. Place them on [背景, e.g. a white plaster wall] in a [排列方式, e.g. asymmetric salon-style arrangement / grid layout].

Consistent lighting across all frames — single light source from [方向, e.g. upper left]. Each frame casts a natural shadow on the wall. Preserve each frame's individual color, texture, and proportions exactly — do not blend or unify the frame colors. The overall composition should feel [风格, e.g. curated, editorial, Japandi-inspired].
🌟
消除割裂感

「harmonize global illumination」但同时加「do NOT change the frame's intrinsic color during harmonization」

🪄
让相框「落地」

「realistic cast shadow on the wall behind the frame」— 相框挂墙时用 cast shadow，不是 contact shadow

4
人物与商品互动
生成真实感的生活场景，人物自然使用/展示产品
公式 4-A · 手部互动（高端感首选） 上传产品图
Lifestyle product photo. Show hands only — [e.g. a woman's hands with natural nails] [e.g. gently holding the wooden picture frame, adjusting it on a shelf].

Setting: [e.g. a bright Scandinavian living room, soft morning light]. The product should be the visual hero. Shallow depth of field, background softly blurred. The frame color, wood grain, and proportions must match the uploaded reference exactly. Photorealistic, editorial quality.
公式 4-B · 全身/半身生活场景 上传产品图
Create a photorealistic lifestyle image. A [e.g. young woman in her 30s, casual linen outfit] is [e.g. hanging a wooden picture frame on a white wall, stepping back to admire it].

Setting: [e.g. a cozy minimalist home interior with warm afternoon light]. The frame from the uploaded image must appear exactly as uploaded — same design, color, wood grain, and proportions. The interaction should feel organic and authentic, not posed. Shot on 35mm lens, slight film grain.
👐
Hands-Only 技巧

「show hands only」避免人脸生成失真，同时让产品保持视觉主角，适合高端电商图

🎬
增加真实感

「candid moment」「organic interaction」「not posed」「shot on 35mm lens」「slight film grain」

🏠
场景描述模板

「bright Scandinavian living room」「cozy minimalist home」「modern Japandi interior」「warm afternoon light」

⚠️
避免人脸失真

如需全脸，加「realistic facial features, natural expression, no AI artifacts」；或直接用背影/侧脸规避

5
保持产品一致性
跨多张图生成时，确保产品外观、细节、比例完全统一

核心逻辑

一致性是电商套图最难的挑战。核心策略：① 每次都上传同一张产品参考图作为锚点；② 用「reference image」明确声明参考关系；③ 列出需要保持一致的具体属性清单；④ 生成变体时只允许改变「一个变量」。

公式 5-A · 多场景套图（核心公式） 每次上传同一产品图
Use the uploaded image as the product reference. The product in all generated images must match this reference exactly in: [frame width, wood grain texture, corner joint details, proportions, color tone, glass reflection].

Generate a lifestyle image of this product in the following scene: [场景描述]. Do not alter the product design in any way. Only the background, lighting, and context should change. Photorealistic.
公式 5-B · 颜色变体系列（橡木/胡桃木多色展示） 上传1张图
Using the uploaded product as the base design, generate [数量] color variants. For each variant, change only the wood color/finish to: [e.g. (1) natural oak — light honey-blonde, (2) walnut — deep warm brown, (3) matte black].

Keep identical across all variants: frame width, profile shape, corner joints, glass reflection, aspect ratio, and overall design. Same studio lighting, same camera angle, same white background. Output all variants in a consistent, comparable style.
公式 5-C · 追加生成（与已有图保持一致） 上传已有图 + 产品图
I have an existing product image series. [Image 1] is an example from the series — use it as the style and lighting reference. [Image 2] is the product reference.

Generate a new image in the exact same visual style as Image 1: same lighting setup, same color grading, same background treatment, same camera angle. The product must match Image 2 exactly — same frame color, wood grain, width, and proportions. New scene: [新场景描述].
📌
锚点图策略

建立一张「主参考图」（白底正面图），每次生成新场景时都上传它，并声明「use as product reference」

相框锁定属性清单

每次生成只允许改变「场景」或「颜色」或「角度」中的一个，多变量同时改会导致产品细节漂移

💡 进阶技巧：迭代优化流程

第一次生成后，不要重新写提示词——直接在对话中追加修改指令效率更高。例如：「The frame color is slightly too dark, make it 20% lighter while keeping everything else the same.」或「The wood grain is missing, add back the straight grain lines.」ChatGPT Image 2 支持在同一对话中持续迭代，充分利用上下文记忆。每次迭代都要上传原始产品参考图，防止 AI 在多轮对话中「遗忘」产品细节。
