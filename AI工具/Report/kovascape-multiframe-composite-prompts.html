📐 使用前：理解A+多框图的核心目标

A+多框展示图的核心不是「展示产品」，而是让买家想象自己家的样子。每张图都要回答一个问题：「这些相框放在我家会是什么效果？」。因此，每个场景都需要：① 真实的家居环境感；② 多框之间的视觉层次；③ 产品细节清晰可辨。本方案所有提示词均已内置「防细节丢失」指令。

▶
通用三步操作流程
所有5种场景均遵循此流程，先读懂再执行
1
准备素材图 上传前必做
产品图：每个相框款式准备一张「白底正面高清图」作为锚点参考图（分辨率越高越好）。
场景图：从 INS/Pinterest 找到目标场景图，确保构图中有明显的「放置相框的空间」（墙面/桌面/层板）。
画芯图：胡桃木配黑白艺术照，橡木配龟背竹线描图——提前准备好画芯素材。
2
执行溶图（按场景选对应公式）核心步骤
上传「场景图 + 所有产品参考图」，使用下方对应场景的提示词公式。关键：每次都要同时上传所有产品参考图，不要只上传场景图。如果一次上传图片数量有限制，优先用「两步法」（先生成单框，再组合）。
3
Canva 后期加字 最终输出
AI生成图导入Canva，叠加文字层。字体使用 Playfair Display（标题）+ Cormorant Garamond（正文）。文字颜色：深色背景用白色，浅色背景用 #1D1B20。不使用亚马逊原生标题字体，避免模块割裂感。

画芯选择策略（已确认）

胡桃木相框 → 画芯使用黑白艺术照（人像/建筑/风景均可）：深棕木色 + 黑白画面 = 高级感对比，避免暖色画芯与木色撞色。
橡木相框 → 画芯使用龟背竹线描图（或植物线稿）：浅金木色 + 绿植线描 = 北欧自然感，与橡木温润质感高度契合。
多框组合时，同一画面内可混用两种画芯，形成视觉节奏感。

公式 1-A · Gallery Wall 完整版（推荐）
上传所有产品图 + 场景图
// 上传：场景图（白墙北欧风室内）+ 各款相框白底图

Create a gallery wall arrangement using the picture frames from the uploaded product images. Place them on the wall in the scene from [场景图].

Arrangement style: asymmetric salon-style layout — mix of portrait and landscape orientations, varying sizes creating visual rhythm. Largest frame ([e.g. 11×14 oak]) anchors the center-left; medium frames ([e.g. 8×10, 5×7]) cluster around it; smallest frames ([e.g. 4×6]) fill the gaps. Spacing between frames: approximately 2–3 inches visually.

PRESERVE for each frame individually:
— Walnut frames: deep warm brown, straight fine grain, satin finish
— Oak frames: light honey-blonde, prominent grain, natural warmth
— Frame width: wide molding profile, do not make frames look thin
— Glass: clear with subtle specular reflection on each frame
— Artwork inside: walnut frames show black and white fine art photography; oak frames show botanical monstera line drawing

Lighting: soft, diffused natural light from the left side. Each frame casts a subtle cast shadow on the wall — consistent shadow direction. Do NOT adapt frame colors to the wall's ambient light.

Background: [e.g. warm white plaster wall, slightly textured, Japandi interior with a glimpse of a linen sofa in the lower corner]. Photorealistic, interior design magazine quality. Shot as if with a 50mm lens, slight depth of field.

上传产品图
Arrange [数量, e.g. 5] picture frames from the uploaded images in a clean gallery wall composition on a pure warm white wall (#F8F6F2).

Layout: [e.g. 2 rows — top row: 3 frames (5×7, 8×10, 5×7); bottom row: 2 frames (4×6, 4×6) centered under the gaps]. Even spacing, aligned by top edge for a structured look.

Each frame must match its uploaded reference exactly — color, grain, width, proportions. Soft studio lighting, single source from upper-left. Realistic wall shadows behind each frame. No background furniture or props — clean, product-focused. High-resolution output suitable for Amazon A+ content (minimum 970px wide).

公式 2-A · 尺寸梯度平铺（主推）
上传所有产品图
Create a flat lay overhead shot of the picture frames from the uploaded images. Arrange them on [背景, e.g. a light natural oak wood surface / a warm linen fabric].

Arrangement: diagonal cascade from top-left to bottom-right, largest frame at top-left, smallest at bottom-right. Frames slightly overlapping at corners to show depth. Each frame tilted 5–10 degrees for a natural, editorial feel — not perfectly parallel.

Preserve for each frame: exact wood color (walnut = deep warm brown; oak = light honey-blonde), grain texture, frame width, glass reflection. Artwork inside: walnut frames → B&W portrait photo; oak frames → monstera botanical line drawing.

Lighting: soft diffused overhead light, no harsh shadows. Subtle soft shadow beneath each frame. Do not blend frame colors into the background surface color.

Camera: directly overhead (90° top-down), no perspective distortion. Clean, minimal, Japandi aesthetic. High-resolution, suitable for Amazon A+ content.
公式 2-B · 橡木 vs 胡桃木材质对比（双列）
上传2款产品图
Create a side-by-side material comparison flat lay. Left side: oak frame (light honey-blonde, prominent grain). Right side: walnut frame (deep warm brown, fine grain). Same size frame for both.

Background: [e.g. split surface — left half light linen, right half dark slate / or unified warm white surface]. A subtle thin dividing line or natural gap between the two frames to emphasize the contrast.

Critical: the color difference between oak and walnut must be clearly visible and accurate. Do not let the background color bleed into the frame colors. Each frame shows its characteristic grain pattern clearly.

Overhead shot, soft even lighting. The image should communicate: 「two distinct materials, one design language」. Clean, editorial, no props.
📸
俯拍防变形关键词

「directly overhead (90° top-down)」「no perspective distortion」「no lens barrel distortion」— 防止框条变形

🎨
背景选择建议

橡木系列配浅色背景（白/米/浅灰）；胡桃木系列配深色背景（深灰/深蓝/黑）；混搭时用中性米色

材质特写 · 框条细节放大
展示木纹/榫卯/玻璃反光等核心卖点 · 适合A+卖点模块

场景定位

「实木质感」「榫卯结构」「高清钢化玻璃」是产品核心卖点，但在整体场景图中细节难以辨认。材质特写图专门解决这个问题，让买家「看得见摸得着」。适合A+卖点模块（M2/M3），配合文字标注使用。

公式 3-A · 木纹质感特写（单框角部）
上传1张产品图
Create an extreme close-up detail shot of the picture frame corner from the uploaded image. Focus on the [walnut / oak] frame's corner joint area.

Show clearly:
— The [walnut: deep warm brown / oak: honey-blonde] wood grain running along the frame molding
— The clean mitered corner joint with grain continuity across the joint
— The satin surface finish — subtle sheen, not glossy
— The frame profile depth — the 3D cross-section of the molding
— The glass edge catching a thin specular highlight

The wood color must be accurate — do not over-saturate or desaturate. Lighting: single raking light from the side to emphasize texture and depth. Macro photography style, shallow depth of field, background softly blurred. Ultra-high detail, no smoothing of grain texture.
公式 3-B · 玻璃反光卖点图
上传1张产品图
Create a lifestyle detail shot showcasing the high-clarity tempered glass of the picture frame from the uploaded image.

Composition: the frame is placed near a [window / soft light source]. The glass surface shows a clean, elegant specular reflection of the light source — demonstrating optical clarity. The reflection should be subtle and refined, not harsh or distracting. The artwork inside the frame remains clearly visible through the glass.

Frame color and proportions must match the uploaded reference exactly. The image should communicate: 「crystal-clear glass that enhances, not obscures, your artwork」. Soft natural lighting, warm interior atmosphere. Close-medium shot, 50mm equivalent focal length.
公式 3-C · 双框材质并排特写（橡木 vs 胡桃木）
上传2张产品图
Create a side-by-side close-up of two frame corners — oak frame on the left, walnut frame on the right. Both frames are the same size and design, showing only the top-right corner of each.

The image should clearly show the contrast between the two wood species:
— Left (Oak): light honey-blonde, prominent open grain, warm golden undertone
— Right (Walnut): deep chocolate brown, fine tight grain, rich warm undertone

Raking side light to emphasize grain texture on both. The gap between the two frames: approximately 1cm, with a thin neutral gray dividing line. Background: [warm white / light gray]. The image communicates: 「same craftsmanship, two distinct personalities」. Macro detail, ultra-sharp, no smoothing.
⚠️ 特写图最常见失败

AI 在特写模式下更容易「美化」木纹，将真实纹理替换为「通用木纹」。防止方法：加「do not replace the grain pattern with a generic wood texture — use the exact grain from the reference image」。

公式 4-A · 尺寸梯度挂墙展示
上传所有尺寸产品图
Create a size progression display of picture frames on a wall. Arrange [数量] frames in a single horizontal row, ordered from smallest (left) to largest (right): [4×6, 5×7, 6×8, 8×10, 11×14].

All frames aligned by their bottom edge, creating a clear ascending size gradient. Spacing between frames: equal, approximately 1.5 inches visually.

Each frame must match its uploaded reference exactly. Alternate materials for visual rhythm: [e.g. walnut, oak, walnut, oak, walnut]. Each frame contains appropriate artwork: walnut → B&W photo, oak → botanical line drawing.

Background: [clean white wall, minimal Japandi interior]. Soft even lighting, subtle wall shadows. The image should clearly communicate the size range of the product line. Straight-on camera angle (no perspective tilt). High-resolution.
公式 4-B · 尺寸对比（含人手参照）
上传产品图
Create a size reference lifestyle shot. Show [2–3 frames of different sizes] leaning against a wall on a console table or shelf.

Include a pair of hands gently holding or adjusting the smallest frame — providing a human scale reference that helps buyers understand the actual size. The hands should be [natural skin tone, clean nails, casual].

All frames must match their uploaded references exactly. The size difference between frames should be clearly visible and proportionally accurate. Setting: [warm minimalist interior, natural light]. The image communicates: 「available in multiple sizes to fit any space」.

生活场景 · 多风格适配展示
展示相框在不同家居风格中的适配性 · 适合A+场景模块

场景定位

「这款相框适合我家的风格吗？」是买家最大的顾虑。多风格展示图直接回答这个问题。每张图对应一种家居风格，展示相框在该风格中的自然融入感。建议规划3种风格：北欧简约 / 中世纪复古 / 现代极简。

公式 5-A · 北欧简约风（Japandi）
上传产品图 + 场景图
Place the frames from the uploaded product images into a Japandi-style living room scene.

Scene elements: white plaster walls, light oak wood furniture, linen textiles, minimal decor, a single potted plant (monstera or olive branch). Natural daylight from a large window, soft and diffused. Color palette: warm whites, natural wood tones, muted sage green accents.

Frame arrangement: [e.g. 3 frames in a small gallery cluster on the wall above a low sideboard]. Preserve each frame's exact color and texture. The overall mood: calm, intentional, lived-in but curated. The frames feel like they belong — not placed, but chosen. Interior design editorial style, 35mm lens, natural grain.
公式 5-B · 中世纪复古风（Mid-Century Modern）
上传产品图 + 场景图
Place the frames from the uploaded product images into a mid-century modern interior scene.

Scene elements: warm walnut wood furniture with tapered legs, mustard yellow or terracotta accent cushions, geometric rug, warm incandescent lighting. Color palette: warm browns, amber, deep teal accents, cream walls.

Frame arrangement: [e.g. 2 walnut frames flanking a vintage-style wall clock, or a row of 3 frames above a credenza]. Walnut frames especially — preserve the deep warm brown color, do not let it blend into the warm-toned furniture. The mood: warm, sophisticated, nostalgic yet fresh. Lifestyle photography style, warm color grade.
公式 5-C · 现代极简风（Modern Minimal）
上传产品图 + 场景图
Place the frames from the uploaded product images into a modern minimalist interior scene.

Scene elements: pure white walls, concrete or light gray flooring, black metal accents (lamp, shelf brackets), very minimal decor — one or two objects maximum. Color palette: white, light gray, black, with the wood frames as the only warm element.

Frame arrangement: [e.g. a single large walnut frame as a statement piece, or 3 frames in a precise grid arrangement]. The frames should provide warmth and organic contrast against the cool, hard surfaces. Preserve frame colors exactly — the contrast between warm wood and cool surroundings is the key visual message. Clean, architectural photography style, straight lines, no distortion.
🏡
风格关键词速查

北欧：「Japandi」「Scandinavian」「wabi-sabi」「organic minimalism」
复古：「mid-century modern」「warm retro」「1960s inspired」
极简：「modern minimal」「architectural」「monochromatic」

💡
防止相框「消失」在场景中

当相框颜色与背景相近时（如橡木 + 木质墙面），加「ensure the frame remains visually distinct from the background — add subtle shadow or contrast」

🔑 执行优先级建议

先做P0（M1/M2/M4）：这三个模块对转化率影响最大。M4多框展示图是整个A+页面的视觉高潮，建议用场景1-A的完整版公式，一次性生成5框以上的Gallery Wall图。

关于模块间衔接：所有图片的光线方向保持一致（统一为左侧光源），色调保持统一（暖白背景系列 or 深色背景系列二选一），避免模块间视觉跳跃。Canva加字时不使用亚马逊原生标题字体，模块间留白统一规划。
